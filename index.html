About the Operator

Hi there! I'm Sebastien Couture, operating as K1TTL from Raleigh, North Carolina. I hold a General class license and enjoy chasing DX and exploring digital modes.

My professional background is in Voice over IP (VoIP) networks and platforms — tangential to amateur radio, but radio itself wasn't on my radar until early 2025, when I started exploring the different means of communication available in emergency and off-grid scenarios. That curiosity quickly turned into a full-fledged hobby: I earned my Technician license in May 2025 and upgraded to General in August 2025.

My main areas of interest within amateur radio are:

  • Emergency and auxiliary communications
  • Digital modes (FT8, FT4, JS8Call, Winlink)
  • Parks On The Air (POTA) / Summits On The Air (SOTA)
  • Amateur radio satellite communications
  • Off-grid and portable station setups
